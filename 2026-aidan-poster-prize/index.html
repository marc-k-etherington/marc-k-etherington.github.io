Aidan wins Poster Prize

Publish date: 27 March 2026

Aidan wins a poster prize at the RSC Organic Chemistry Regional Meeting held at Newcastle University. He was presenting his latest results on our new approach to donor-acceptor chromophores for thermally activated delayed fluorescence. Well done Aidan!
