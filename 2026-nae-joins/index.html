Nae Aota from the University of Osaka joins the group for her 3-month visit

Publish date: 22 May 2026

Nae Aota joins us from the group of Youhei Takeda at the University of Osaka as part of the JST ASPIRE grant. Nae will be with us for just over 3 months and we are excited to see what science emerges as a result of the visit.
